January is the perfect time to set New Year’s resolutions for seniors. Together with adult children, seniors can build New Year’s resolutions that reflect on relationships and meaningful connection. Aging brings unique challenges, and by committing to thoughtful goals, seniors and their loved ones can build feelings of being valued, loved, and connected.
